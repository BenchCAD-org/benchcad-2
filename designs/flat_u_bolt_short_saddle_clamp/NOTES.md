# Evidence and scope notes

This family is limited to the STAUFF `FB+RUK` fixed-point assembly shown in the
Issue #91 evidence set: manual-case evidence 01-03 and 13-15 only. Evidence
assigned to the round-bar proposal (#92) was not read or used.

The 25 parameter rows are complete coupled rows from STAUFF Catalogue 1
(English, 06/2026), pages 150-151. Drawing 01 maps L1 to channel length, L2 to
flat-U leg pitch, H1/H2 to arch top/spring-line, H3 to profile top-web thickness,
B1 to flat-bar width/thickness and B2 x H4 to the channel section. Drawing 13
maps L3/B3 to saddle plan dimensions, L4/D2 to its two-hole interface, H5 to
bottom-to-groove-low thickness, H6 to total height, and H7 to the downward
interface boss. DN40's stated 90-degree L4 stagger is preserved.

Only profile side-wall thickness, clearances and the ellipse representation of
the undimensioned bend profile are deterministic `proportion` geometry. The
top-web thickness is the published H3 value. Hex nuts and lower bolt heads use
ISO 4032 / ISO 4017 envelopes and ISO 261 / ISO 68-1 coarse-thread basic bore
dimensions, following the standard-components demo. The product view shows
round threaded ends beneath the flat-bar legs: these ends and the ISO 4017
lower-bolt shanks carry visible coarse external threads, while the two upper
nuts carry matching internal grooves. RUK D2 and U-profile passages remain
unthreaded clearance interfaces. Washers, bend fillets, draft and recesses
remain omitted.

Novelty is the complete seven-solid fixed-point assembly: flat-steel U-piece,
short RUK saddle, DIN 1026 U-profile, two nuts and two lower hex-head bolts.
The bare U-bolt is not claimed as novel. Heldout #155 may overlap the DIN 3570
/ bare-U-bolt portion; that overlap remains a review risk and must not be
resolved by citing or importing evidence from #92.

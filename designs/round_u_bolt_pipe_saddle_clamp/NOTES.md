# Evidence and modeling notes

Only Issue #92 evidence `04`-`12` and `16`-`18` was used. Issue #91 evidence
`01`-`03` and `13`-`15` was not opened, read, or used.

- `04`, `07`: RB+RUK/RUL drawings establish the round U-bolt, saddle
  interface, parallel legs, dimensions, and coaxial nut arrangement.
- `05`, `08`: 31 complete RB rows provide `D1/A/L1/H1/H2/G` and the
  variant-specific `H3/H4` values.
- `06`, `09`: product views establish the installed pose and round-rod form.
- `10`-`12`: RUK drawing, A-coupled table, and product view establish its
  compact short-saddle topology, two solid D2 bottom bosses at `+/-L3/2`,
  and their downward H7 projection.
- `16`-`18`: RUL drawing, A-coupled table, and product view establish its
  extended long-saddle topology and two D4 through-holes at `+/-L3/2`.

`A` is the U-bolt clear inside width and `L1=A+G` is its leg-center spacing;
leg axes are therefore at `+/-L1/2`. The U-bolt leg end is located from H3.
With saddle bottom as `z=0`, the coaxial pipe-groove center is at
`H4=H5+D1/2`, so the groove's lowest point is exactly H5. No arbitrary 2%
offset is used for any of these sourced interfaces.

H2 is retained and row-validated as the sourced threaded height measured from
each leg end. Both legs use visible ISO 261 coarse-pitch / ISO 68-1 simplified
60-degree external threads over H2; all four ISO 4032 nut envelopes carry the
matching simplified internal groove. RUK D2 features remain solid locating
bosses and RUL D4 features remain unthreaded clearance holes.

The contribution's novelty is not the bare round U-bolt (the corpus already
has DIN 3570-like U-bolt geometry). It is the two discrete RUK/RUL saddle
topologies and the complete six-solid installed assembly: one round-steel
U-bolt, one saddle, and four nuts.

The catalog does not fully dimension bend-center radius, saddle draft/radii or
running clearance. Deterministic construction values for those details are
therefore labeled `proportion`. Nuts use ISO 4032 envelopes and ISO 261 /
ISO 68-1 coarse-thread basic minor bores and matching helical grooves following
the standard-components demo. Pipe, substrate, washers, preload, and
deformation are excluded.

## Heldout overlap risk

Heldout family #155 may overlap the generic pipe-support or U-bolt envelope.
This implementation should remain differentiated by the sourced RUK/RUL
variant topology and named six-component assembly, not by claiming novelty for
the round U-bolt itself. The heldout geometry cannot be inspected here, so
reviewer confirmation remains required before publication.
